# Decision: the moon phase is tied to progress (in play mode)

**Context.** Open question since the start: is `moonPhase` player-controlled, time-based, or tied to progress?

**Options.**
1. Player-controlled (a slider): good for the atlas/labs, no story.
2. Time-based: the night passes on a clock; simple, but the player's actions don't matter.
3. Tied to progress: the moon rises as the player discovers the world.

**Choice.** Both, by mode: in the **labs** the moon is a slider (Moon & atmosphere); in **play mode** it's tied to progress: each reef woken raises the moon (5 reefs = full moon), plus a slow drift with time (at most 5 minutes) so the night always ends.

**Why.** Discovery is the reward (no scores), so discovery should move the night forward. It also makes the core tension playable: the more you wake, the brighter the moon, and the more your own glow is washed out. Waking the world costs you your visibility.
