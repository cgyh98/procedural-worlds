## Learned
- Separating *what a thing is* (body + motion: pulse, tilt, follow-the-leader chains) from *how it's drawn* (glow vs. painted) makes a style a swappable option: the same simulation feeds either renderer.
- A big mixed commit makes rolling back harder: the game commit also held voxel tweaks and midterm docs, so a plain revert would have lost them. Smaller commits per topic from now on.
