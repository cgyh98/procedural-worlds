### Learned
- Advection: `position += velocity × dt`.
- Curl of a noise potential is divergence-free (no clumping); finite differences approximate derivatives. Verified numerically.
- Write per-frame data into the geometry's buffers (via the ref), not into memoized arrays; React's lint rules treat memo values as read-only.

### Learned
- The total excitation a plankton gets from one pass doesn't depend on speed (it cancels out); speed changes how many plankton you light and how fresh the trail is.
- Bugs found through the screenshots: excited plankton kept glowing after wrapping (trail looped to the front); near-camera points drawn huge (fixed with a small shader edit via `onBeforeCompile`); disturbance radius 2 made a tube, not a trail.
- Headless screenshots run at ~9 fps without a GPU, so they show the mechanic only roughly.
